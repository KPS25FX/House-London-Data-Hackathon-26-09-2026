      The evidence

      Who decides what gets built: demand, or the people already here?

      If building followed need, the places people most want to move to would build most. They don't. Each finding below compares the top and bottom thirds of London's seats, with what it means for policy.

      Each dot is a seat. Further right: more people trying to move in. Higher: more residents worried about housing. Colour: area type, as on the map.

      The decision each area type calls for

      An MP backs more homes when the voters who reward it outweigh the voters who punish it. Market demand says whether homes would actually get built; resident concern says which way the MP's own voters lean.
